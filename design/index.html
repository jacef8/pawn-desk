Three designs

Same numbers, same job, three different shapes. Open each on the phone you'd actually use. Tell me a letter — or which parts of which.

A · Wallet Your Dribbble reference, taken literally. A vivid gradient card carries one huge figure, circular action buttons sit directly under it, and everything else is an icon-led row with the value on the right. Front page is a balance screen: today's numbers, then a feed of what you've priced. Most like the reference you sent → B · Plain No cards at all. No panels, no glass, no borders. Hairline rules separate zones and type does the whole job — the offer is set at a size nothing competes with. Colour appears twice: on the number, and on whatever you can press. The quietest, and the fastest to read → C · Ticket Shaped like what the job produces. One tall ticket per item, torn along a perforated edge, set in ledger monospace, with the offer stamped across the bottom. Reads top to bottom in the order you'd say it to a customer. The only one that looks like a pawn shop →
Two honest caveats. These are design samples with real numbers, not the working app — nothing in them is wired up. And the webfonts are blocked where I build, so I have never seen these in Sora either; on your phone they should look better than they do on mine.
