# Product Research worksheet — what sold vs what was asked

Fill in the last column. Twelve lookups, about fifteen minutes.

Every price in The Pawn Desk that came out of the eBay harvest is an ASKING
price. eBay declined the Marketplace Insights application on 23 Sep 2026, so
that stays true: this worksheet is no longer a stopgap while an application
is pending, it is the only sold data the desk has. Asks read high: the
overpriced listing that sat six months is still in the index, the one that
sold in a day is gone. Nobody knows how high. These twelve say how high.

## Settings — set these once, they stick

In Seller Hub: **Research** tab -> **Product Research**.

- **Date range:** Last 90 days
- **Condition:** Used
- **Marketplace:** eBay US (United States)
- **Format:** All (Auction and Buy It Now both count)

## What to write down

The number you want is **Average sold price**, near the top of the results
panel. Not the range, not the "average shipping", not the active-listing
figure. If Product Research shows fewer than about 10 sales in 90 days, widen the
date range to a year and write "1yr" beside the number.

## Why twelve and not one

One item tells you nothing — any single model can be odd. Twelve across four
categories says whether the gap between asks and sales is steady. If it is
steady, the 220 harvested rows can be corrected by that factor and merged,
and the price book roughly doubles. If it is all over the place, they cannot
be corrected and they stay out until there is real sold data.

Either answer is worth the fifteen minutes. The one that is not worth
anything is guessing.
